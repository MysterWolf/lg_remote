DPad Pilot Privacy Policy

The short version

DPad Pilot does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What the app does

DPad Pilot connects to your LG WebOS TV over your local WiFi network using the SSAP protocol. All communication happens entirely on your local network. No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

What we don't collect

  • No account or login required
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No personal information of any kind

Local storage

The app stores your saved TV names and IP addresses locally on your device using Android shared preferences. This data never leaves your device.
